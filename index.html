A newly opened studio, backed by 12 years of fine jewelry craftsmanship — specializing in custom jewelry, precision stone setting, and detailed hand engraving.

Each piece that leaves our bench is designed, set and engraved with the same patience, whether it is a first custom ring or a treasured piece that needs care.

What we do

Custom Design

We create custom jewelry based on your style, ideas, and vision. We would be delighted to design the ring you have always imagined and bring your vision to life.

Stone Setting

Precision stone setting for diamonds and gemstones, including pavé, micro pavé, prong setting, and custom setting work.

Hand Engraving

Jewelry Repair
